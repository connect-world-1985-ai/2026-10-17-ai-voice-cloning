Your voice, synthesised

A vendor-neutral, evidence-based guide to making an AI narrate lessons, tutorials and explainer videos in your own voice, in English and Mandarin Chinese, including your voice in the language you recorded less of. Written for a software engineer who is new to speech AI. State of the field: 17 October 2026.

Ten findings

  1. It works, and quickly. One to two minutes of clean audio gives a usable instant clone with the leading cloud services; 30 minutes to 3 hours gives a professional clone. Open models clone from 3–15 seconds. Verified (vendor docs, READMEs)
  2. Best cloud quality an individual can buy: ElevenLabs Eleven v4, Alibaba Qwen-Audio 3.1 and Cartesia Sonic 3.6 sit in the top tier of the independent cloned-voice arena (English voices only). Verified (page 2)
  3. Price per hour of narration ranges from about $0.82 (Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS) to about $4–6 (Eleven v4, MiniMax HD) in English; Mandarin is 3–4× cheaper per hour when billed per character. Verified prices · per-hour figures Inference
  4. Open models are as good on paper. VoxCPM2, Fun-CosyVoice 3 and Qwen3-TTS (all Apache-2.0) report speaker similarity near or above the level of two real recordings of the same person on the standard benchmark. Verified (self-reported, page 3)
  5. Licences decide more than quality. F5-TTS, XTTS-v2, Fish Audio S2-Pro and others have non-commercial weights. Code and weights licences often differ. Verified
  6. Cross-lingual cloning costs similarity. Benchmarks show about 10 points lower similarity when the reference and the output are in different languages. Record a reference in each language. Verified · advice Inference
  7. Mandarin needs pronunciation control. Polyphones (行 háng / xíng) and tones are the main failure; IndexTTS and CosyVoice accept pinyin overrides; ElevenLabs v4 accepts dictionaries and inline IPA but not SSML breaks. Verified (page 7)
  8. A course is a pipeline, not a clip. Normalise, chunk, synthesise, check with ASR, regenerate, assemble, align, caption. An agent can automate almost all of it; ears are still needed for flagged chunks and emphasis. Practice (page 8)
  9. Disclose it. EU AI Act Art. 50 has applied since 2 Aug 2026; a cloned-voice tutorial very likely counts as a deep fake. YouTube exempts your own cloned voice, but Udemy, TikTok and Chinese rules require labels. Verified rules · application Inference (page 9)
  10. Protect yourself. Turn off bank voice-ID; vendors take broad, often perpetual licences to your recordings; watermarks can be removed. Verified
If you read only one thing Run the comparison on page 11 with ElevenLabs v4, Gemini voice replication and one Apache-licensed local model (VoxCPM2 or Fun-CosyVoice 3). No benchmark measures how much a clone sounds like you in Mandarin; only your own test can. Inference

PracticeCommon practice or rule of thumb in the field, widely documented but not a single authoritative source.
InferenceMy own reasoning from the evidence: recommendations, thresholds, interpretations.
UnverifiedOnly secondary sources, conflicting sources, or not checkable during this research.
